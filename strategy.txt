Yes. For your **first ₹10k–₹15k test**, I would keep it simple:

**1 ad creative = 1 image.**

Don't make carousels yet. Don't make 5-slide stories. Don't make complicated funnels.

Your job is to answer:

> Which single message makes a stranger stop scrolling and click?

---

### Your initial testing structure:

## Campaign: BhavishAI Launch

### Ad Set A — Broad Audience

* India
* Age 21–40
* Instagram Feed/Reels/Stories

Ads inside:

* Ad 1: Career/Life Questions Image
* Ad 2: Exact Birth Chart / Personalized Report Image

### Ad Set B — Astrology Interest Audience

Same 3 images:

* Ad 1: Questions
* Ad 2: Personalization

| --------- | --------------- |
| Broad     | Questions       |
| Broad     | Personalization |
| Broad     | Comparison      |

| Astrology | Personalization |
| Astrology | Comparison      |

---

## Why not a carousel?

Because with your budget:

* ₹500/day total
* ₹250 per audience

You need data quickly.

If you use a 5-image carousel, you won't know:

* Did the first slide work?
* Did people swipe?
* Which message attracted them?

A single image gives cleaner data.

---

## My recommendation for the first image (highest confidence)

A dark premium image with just:

```
Will I get promoted?

Will I get married?

Should I switch careers?

Your birth chart may reveal the answers.

Personalized 20-page Kundli Report
₹299 • Ready in 60 seconds
```

No logo at the top.
No 10 bullet points.
No "AI-powered Swiss Ephemeris" headline.

Your ad has about **1 second to interrupt a thumb scroll**.

---

### One small trick I would do

Make each image in **two formats**:

1. **1080 × 1080** (Instagram Feed)
2. **1080 × 1920** (Stories/Reels)

The message should be identical. Just adapt the layout.

---

If I were launching BhavishAI with ₹15,000, I would probably spend **80% of my creative effort on the first "questions" ad**, because psychologically it hits the exact reason someone buys astrology: **uncertainty about the future**.
